📊 E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI
📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales data using Microsoft Power BI to understand overall sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product preferences, and business operations.

An interactive, two-page dashboard was developed to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify sales trends, compare business performance, and generate meaningful business insights.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of e-commerce operations through interactive visualizations, charts, geographical maps, and slicers.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies
Microsoft Excel: Initial data handling and preparation.
Power Query: Data cleaning and transformation.
Microsoft Power BI: Dashboard development and visualization.
DAX: Calculated measures and KPI development.
📈 Dashboard Features

The dashboard consists of two interactive pages:

1. Sales Performance Dashboard
KPI Cards: Displays Total Sales, Total Profit, and Total Discount.
Delivery Partner Analysis: Compares sales across DTDC, Xpressbees, Shadowfax, Delhivery, Blue Dart, Ecom Express, and India Post.
Sales by Channel: Compares invoice amounts and profit across Mobile App, Website, Marketplace, and Social Commerce.
Monthly Sales Trends: Tracks sales performance across all 12 months.
Payment Mode Analysis: Analyzes customer payment preferences across UPI, Debit Card, COD, Credit Card, Net Banking, and Wallet.

2. Sales Analysis Dashboard
Zone & Age Classification: Analyzes sales across different geographical zones and customer age classifications.
Sales by State: Displays geographical sales distribution across Indian states using an interactive map.
Sales by Category: Compares sales across Electronics, Mobiles & Accessories, Home & Kitchen, Fashion, Sports & Fitness, Beauty & Personal Care, Grocery & Gourmet, and Books & Stationery.
Order Date Analysis: Allows users to explore sales within a selected date range.
Weekend vs. Weekday Analysis: Provides an option to compare weekday and weekend orders.
